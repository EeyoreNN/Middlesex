APP STORE DESCRIPTION FOR MIDDLESEX APP
=========================================

SUBTITLE (30 characters max):
Your Middlesex School app


PROMOTIONAL TEXT (170 characters max - optional):
Get the latest schedules, menus, announcements, and sports scores for Middlesex School. Your unofficial Middlesex companion.


FULL DESCRIPTION:
Stay connected with everything happening at Middlesex School with the unofficial Middlesex app.

FEATURES

Schedule Management
• View your personalized class schedule
• Import schedules using AI-powered photo scanning
• Track X-block days and special schedules
• Request custom classes

Dining Hall Menus
• Browse daily breakfast, lunch, and dinner menus
• Filter by dietary preferences (vegetarian, vegan)
• View allergen information
• Check what's being served before you head to the dining hall

School Announcements
• Receive important school-wide announcements
• Get grade-specific notifications
• Stay updated on events, meetings, and deadlines
• Never miss important information

Sports & Athletics
• Follow your favorite Middlesex teams
• Get real-time score updates with Live Activities
• View upcoming games and results
• Track athletic events across all sports

Campus Weather
• Check current weather conditions on campus
• Plan your day with accurate Concord, MA weather

ABOUT THIS APP

This is an independent app developed by Nicholas Noon. It is NOT an official Middlesex School application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Middlesex School.

All data is securely stored using Apple's CloudKit service with end-to-end encryption. Your privacy is our priority.
